Movie Roulette Tuesday — Support

Movie Roulette Tuesday is a free companion app for Movie Roulette Tuesday: The Podcast, where Rachael, Karl, and Matt meet each week for dinner, a movie, and a discussion. Spin the roulette to pick a movie, browse episodes, vote in community polls, and rate movies.

Compatible with: iPhone and iPad.

What's new in each beta build

FAQ

How do I spin? Open the app and tap the roulette wheel (Spin). Your result is saved to your spin history.

Where do episodes play? The app doesn't play audio itself. When you tap an episode, a link opens it in your browser or podcast app.

What data does the app keep, and how do I reset it? Spin history, stats, and your personal ratings stay on your device. Deleting the app clears them. Poll votes and community movie ratings are stored anonymously in Apple CloudKit. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account? No. The app has no accounts or sign-ups.

Report a bug or get help

Email theMrtPodcast@gmail.com and include: - Your device model and iOS/iPadOS version - The app version (from the App Store page) - What happened, the steps to reproduce it, and a screenshot if you can
