Install Movie Roulette Tuesday on Android

Movie Roulette Tuesday for Android is coming to Google Play. Until then, you can install it directly from our GitHub page. It's free, has no ads and no account, and needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Download Movie Roulette Tuesday 1.1 for Android (APK, 3.4 MB) · Release notes · What's new

1. Download

On your Android phone or tablet, tap the download link above (or open the latest release and tap mrt-android-1.1-3.apk under Assets). If your browser says the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.

2. Allow the install

When the download finishes, tap the notification (or open the file from your Files or Downloads app) and tap Install.

The first time, Android asks you to allow the app you used to open the file (for example Chrome, Samsung Internet, or Files) to install apps:

  • Tap Settings in the prompt.
  • Turn on Allow from this source (on some phones: Install unknown apps).
  • Go back and tap Install.

You can turn that switch off again afterwards in Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps.

3. If Play Protect shows a warning

Google Play Protect may say it doesn't recognize the app, because it isn't on Google Play yet. Tap More details, then Install anyway. If it offers to send the app for a scan, you can say yes. You don't need to turn Play Protect off.

Check the download (optional)

Updating

Download the newest APK from the releases page and install it over the old one. Don't uninstall first: uninstalling erases your ratings, watchlist, and spin history.

Want updates automatically? Install Obtainium (free and open source), tap Add App, paste https://github.com/movieroulettetuesday/mrt-android, and tap Add. It tells you when there's a new version.

Moving to Google Play later

Every version, including the future Google Play version, is signed with the same certificate, so the Play version can update the app you installed here. Your data stays put. We'll announce it here and on the podcast when it's live.

Trouble?

"App not installed" or "package conflicts with an existing package": you probably have a test build of the app installed, which can't be updated by this version. Email us before uninstalling, since that erases your data.

Email theMrtPodcast@gmail.com and include your device model, Android version, the app version, what happened, and a screenshot if you can.
