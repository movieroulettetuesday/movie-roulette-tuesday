Movie Roulette Tuesday — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 Developer: Matthew Studley Contact: theMrtPodcast@gmail.com

Movie Roulette Tuesday is a free companion app for Movie Roulette Tuesday: The Podcast. The app has no ads and no in-app purchases, and it does not sell or share your data.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups. The app never asks for your email or contact info. The only thing you can choose to type is an optional credit name on a movie suggestion (see below).
  • No third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. (On Android, Google's on-device AI library sends Google basic usage metrics; see Android app below.)
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for tracking.

Data that stays on your device

Your spin history, stats, watchlist, watched list, settings, and optional local reminders are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app removes them.

Network connections the app makes

  • Buzzsprout (rss.buzzsprout.com, storage.buzzsprout.com): to download the podcast's public RSS feed and episode artwork.
  • Apple iTunes Lookup API: to look up the podcast's public listing. The only thing sent is the show's ID, never anything about you.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): to download the public movie catalog (movies.json), including where each movie is streaming.
  • TMDB (image.tmdb.org): to show streaming-service logos. The app sends no personal data. TMDB's servers only see your IP address, as with any web request.
  • Apple CloudKit (public database): for community polls, movie ratings, and movie suggestions.
    • Polls and ratings: when you vote or rate, the app stores the poll and option you chose, or the movie and your half-star rating (sharing your ratings is off by default), together with a SHA-256 hash of your iCloud user record ID. The hash stops duplicate votes. Your raw iCloud ID is never stored, and no profile is built. These records are stored without your name, email, or Apple ID, and we can't see any of those.
    • Movie suggestions: if you use Suggest a Movie, the app sends the movie title you typed, an optional category, an optional note, an optional credit name, and the app version. It does not attach your Apple ID, email, or device identifier. Suggestions are limited to one every ten minutes. Please don't put personal information in these fields.
    • Apple's privacy policy covers CloudKit: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Email option: Send by email opens the Mail app with a draft to the podcast. Nothing is sent until you send it, and then your email provider's policy applies.

Links to episodes or other websites open only when you tap them. Once a site opens, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Android app

The Android app follows this policy, with these differences:

  • Stays on your device: your spin history, personal ratings, watchlist, watched list, and settings are stored only in the app's private storage. They aren't included in Android cloud backup. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Network connections: the same public sources as above: Buzzsprout (podcast feed and episode artwork), the Apple iTunes Lookup API (the show's public listing), GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, the public movie catalog), and TMDB (image.tmdb.org, streaming-service logos). The app sends nothing about you; these servers only see your IP address, as with any web request.
  • No community features yet: polls, shared ratings, and in-app suggestions are turned off in the Android app, so it doesn't connect to Apple CloudKit. Suggest a Movie opens your email app with a draft; nothing is sent until you send it.
  • Pick for Me and question search (Gemini Nano): on supported devices, these use Google's Gemini Nano model, which runs on your device through Android's AICore service. What you type and the model's answers are processed on the device and aren't sent to us or to Google's servers. Google's ML Kit library, which the app uses to reach Gemini Nano, sends Google usage and diagnostic metrics (for example device model, Android version, app version, a per-installation identifier, latency, and error codes) to maintain the feature and detect abuse. It doesn't send what you type. See ML Kit Terms & Privacy.
  • System features, on your device: home screen shortcuts, the Spin of the Day widget, Android system search (movie titles are added to the on-device search index on Android 12+), spoken answers through your device's text-to-speech, and the optional new-episode alert (a local notification after the app checks the public podcast feed).
  • No accounts, ads, or advertising ID: the app removes the Android advertising ID permission. The only permission it asks you for is notifications, and only if you turn on new-episode alerts.
  • Installing from GitHub: if you download the app from our GitHub releases page, GitHub's privacy policy covers that download.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete on-device data by deleting (on Android, uninstalling) the app. To ask about a poll, rating, or suggestion record, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
