Movie Roulette Tuesday — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026 Developer: Matthew Studley Contact: mrt-release@mail.grokbot.com

Movie Roulette Tuesday is a free companion app for Movie Roulette Tuesday: The Podcast. The app has no ads and no in-app purchases, and it does not sell or share your data.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, names, emails, or contact info.
  • No third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for tracking.

Data that stays on your device

Your spin history, stats, and personal movie ratings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app removes them.

Network connections the app makes

  • Buzzsprout (rss.buzzsprout.com, storage.buzzsprout.com): to download the podcast's public RSS feed and episode artwork.
  • Apple iTunes Lookup API: to look up the podcast's public listing. The only thing sent is the show's ID, never anything about you.
  • Apple CloudKit (public database): for community polls and movie ratings. When you vote or rate, the app stores the poll and option you chose, or the movie and your star rating, together with a SHA-256 hash of your iCloud user record ID. The hash stops duplicate votes. Your raw iCloud ID is never stored, and no profile is built. These records are stored without your name, email, or Apple ID, and we can't see any of those. Apple's privacy policy covers CloudKit: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Links to episodes or other websites open only when you tap them. Once a site opens, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete on-device data by deleting the app. To ask about anonymous poll or rating records, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
